# Amin Live TV v3.9.2

Adds **14 official Sports collections with 49 verified full matches/event recordings**: FA Cup, J.League, FIBA, FIBA 3x3, Volleyball World, BWF TV, World Table Tennis, Australian Open, US Open, GTWorld, Formula E archives, ONE Championship, World Taekwondo and FIDE. Existing Persian Chess joins Sports with its original content.

Sports now includes reviewed full matches and event replays from official publishers. «شطرنج فارسی» moves into the same Sports grid with its existing ID and playlist. Native live stations appear first, followed by official YouTube collections. There are no new tabs or subcategories.

Catalogue revision 12 supplies the selections. A small app update is required: previous versions cannot display YouTube programmes in Sports. Version 3.9.2 accepts bounded lists of reviewed video IDs through YouTube's official embedded player. This avoids exposing entire uploads feeds containing shorts, trailers, unavailable events and highlights. Future selections can change remotely without another APK.

Older clients can still accept revision 12: they skip the new Sports collections and retain Persian Chess in its original category until upgraded. Package `com.enigma.aminlivetv`, the signing certificate and all viewing-data formats are preserved. Version code is 18. No dependencies, subtitle engine, home-screen, VPN or Amin Player changes are included.

Only videos that actually rendered non-ad video frames in Amin TV's Android TV emulator from Portugal are included. This is a spot playback check, not a promise of future rights or a full-duration viewing test. YouTube can change regional or embedding permissions. Blocked embeds retain the existing official YouTube-app fallback.

See the [source review and test report](../sports-sources-2026-11-24.md) and [per-video verification evidence](../sports-verification-2026-11-24.json) for accepted sources, rejected candidates, counts and device-test limits.

**The Xiaomi box was not installed, updated or interrupted for this release.** Physical Xiaomi playback of the expanded Sports catalogue remains unverified. Updating on that device is left to the owner.
