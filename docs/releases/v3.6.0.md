# Amin Live TV v3.6.0

**سریال‌های ایرانی** now has 12 complete series and two clearly labeled partial series, with 266 free episodes. The seven new complete runs are آبان (17), ابله (14), سال‌های دور از خانه (15), اجل معلق (20), مو به مو (12), اهل ایران (14) and گنج مظفر (12).

**قسمت‌های موجود** marks بامداد خمار (26 of 27, with the second-season finale reserved for members) and از یاد رفته (17 of 18, with episode 1 blocked from embedding).

The library shows a thumbnail for each title. While watching, choose **فهرست قسمت‌ها** to return directly to the episode list. Viewing positions are stored by title, episode and video, and resume uses the official embedded YouTube player. Playback in the separate YouTube app does not record a position in Amin Live TV.

## Verification

All 266 episodes passed signed-out YouTube watch-page checks for Portugal, including publisher identity and embedding permission. The Android TV emulator played the first listed episode of each of the nine additions, displayed a remotely shuffled catalogue in numerical order and returned from playback to the episode list using the remote. The pilot saved at 140.39 seconds and resumed at 140.49 seconds after a force-stop. Restart and next-episode playback passed. The 12 unit tests and debug and release lint passed.

The APK uses package `com.enigma.aminlivetv` and the v3.5.1 signing certificate. The signed build upgraded v3.5.1 in place on the Android TV emulator and played the existing saved episode. Device testing used the emulator only.
