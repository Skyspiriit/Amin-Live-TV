Amin Live TV 0.3.2 adds free full-length movies, TV series and more documentaries from official YouTube channels.

- **New on the home screen:** فیلم سینمایی (Mosfilm classics, FilmRise true stories and comedy, Movie Central Christmas films, Shout! drama and film noir) and سریال کامل (*Unsolved Mysteries* with Robert Stack, *Greatest Prison Escapes*, Paul O'Grady's *For the Love of Dogs*).
- **More documentaries:** Free Documentary, its History, Nature, Ocean and Space channels, FD Adventure, ENDEVR, DW Documentary, Timeline, GEDEON DOC and Real Wild, plus Royal Institution lectures. These also reach 0.3.1 through the catalogue.
- **How it plays:** one OK starts the playlist. Owners who block embedding open in the YouTube app at that exact video.

Tested on the Android TV emulator (Google TV, Android 11). Not yet tested on the Xiaomi box. Install it with the update button in the app.
