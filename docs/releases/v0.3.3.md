Amin Live TV 0.3.3 adds automatic Persian subtitles to documentaries, films and series from YouTube.

- **How it works:** one OK starts the programme with Persian subtitles already on. If the video has Persian captions, those are shown; otherwise YouTube's own player translates the English captions into Persian, in sync with the speech.
- **Where:** مستند, حیات وحش, طبیعت, علم و فضا, فیلم سینمایی and سریال کامل. Live news and TV channels are unchanged.
- **Limits:** videos without English or Persian captions play without subtitles. The translation is YouTube's machine translation, so names and idioms are sometimes off.

Tested on the Android TV emulator (Google TV, Android 11). Install it with the update button in the app.
