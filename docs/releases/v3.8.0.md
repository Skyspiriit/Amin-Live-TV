# Amin Live TV v3.8.0

The home screen now features Persian fantasy artwork and clear topic rows. Press **رنگ‌ها** to choose among باغ زمرد، رویای فیروزه، قصر لاجورد، کهکشان بنفشه، شفق یاقوت and کویر زعفران. Large Persian text, visible focus borders and D-pad scrolling make every section accessible.

## Added channels

- **مافیا و مسابقه** includes Don Complex, Win Club, Honar Aval’s شام ایرانی and KhandeBazi’s بگو بخند.
- **سینما و فانتزی** includes Never miss a Point, Filmbaz and Ehsan Mansoori.
- **دنیای خودرو** includes Top Gear.
- **شطرنج** includes PersianChess.
- **آیین و مداحی** includes SeratRecords.
- **مستند** and **حیات وحش** include National Geographic and Nat Geo Animals, using the saved documentary-round player.

## Added series

جدی نگیرید (21), شوخی کردم (24) and اکسیر (15) have complete numbered archive runs. املت has 13 episodes marked **قسمت‌های موجود**. نسخه آزمایشی has 14 episodes marked **در حال پخش**. The five titles add 87 full episodes using stable episode and video IDs.

All 87 episodes and three samples from each new programme tile passed public playback and embedding checks from Portugal. Archive licensing limitations are recorded in the source report.

## Verification

The Android TV emulator played all 12 new programme tiles and the first episode of each new series. Episode progress was saved and automatic captions stayed off in the Persian-series player. D-pad checks reached every home category. Each of the six themes saved successfully, including after an app restart.

All 15 unit tests passed. Debug and release lint passed. The signed APK upgraded v3.7.0 in place on the emulator, retained its existing episode bookmark and resumed playback. Its package is com.enigma.aminlivetv and its signing certificate matches v3.7.0. Device tests used the Android TV emulator.
