Amin Live TV 3.4 gives far more documentaries, films and series Persian subtitles.

- **Automatic captions now work:** most YouTube documentaries only have YouTube's automatic English captions. The player never switched those on by itself, so these programmes showed no subtitles. 3.4 asks for them directly, and YouTube's player translates them into Persian in sync with the speech.
- **Better choices per video:** the video's own Persian track first, then translations of human-written English, other human captions, and automatic captions. Persian-language programmes get no subtitles.
- **New and replaced programmes (catalogue revision 7, also visible in 3.3):** Netflix's *Our Planet*, Bob Ross's *The Joy of Painting*, DW documentaries, Free Documentary nature series, Kenny Rogers films and Shout! documentaries replace sources that blocked embedding, are blocked in Portugal or had no captions. Persian-language documentaries from BBC Persian and Manoto are new.
- **Not possible:** live news channels. YouTube doesn't translate live captions.

Tested on the Android TV emulator (Android 11 and Android 14). Install it with the update button in the app. The release tag is `v3.4.0` so that 0.3.3 also finds it.
