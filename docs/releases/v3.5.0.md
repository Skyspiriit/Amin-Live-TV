# Amin Live TV 3.5

- **سریال‌های ایرانی** opens a Persian library with five complete series and 119 episodes.
- Resume an episode at the saved position, restart it, choose a different one or go to the next episode when it finishes.
- The official YouTube player plays stable episode IDs. Progress stays on the device. Videos that block embedding can open in the official YouTube TV app, where Amin Live TV cannot track the viewing position.
