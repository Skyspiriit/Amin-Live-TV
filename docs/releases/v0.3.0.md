Amin Live TV 0.3.0 adds documentaries, wildlife, nature and science, and plays international news with one OK press.

- **New on the home screen:** مستند, حیات وحش, طبیعت and علم و فضا, below the existing four categories. Movies & Series is unchanged.
- **Documentaries:** Persiana Docs (native), ARTE, Earth Touch and NHK WORLD-JAPAN.
- **Wildlife:** Love Nature 24/7, WildEarth safaris, Earth Touch and Love Nature oceans.
- **Nature:** Stingray Naturescape scenery and water sounds, ARTE nature and Love Nature habitats.
- **Science and space:** NASA live from space, James Webb and Mars rover, and NHK on climate.
- **News:** DW, France 24, Euronews, Al Jazeera and CBS play inside the app from each broadcaster's official YouTube channel. BBC Persian, Sky News, ABC News Live and CNN Headlines open that exact live broadcast in the official YouTube app, because their owners don't allow embedding.
- **Labels:** tiles marked «یوتیوب رسمی» use YouTube's official player. Unmarked tiles are native streams.
- **Persiana Sports 1–4** stay hidden. None of the candidate streams were reachable from Portugal.

Tested on the Android TV emulator (Google TV, Android 11, 1080p). It has not been tested on the Xiaomi box yet. See [VERIFICATION-0.3.0.md](https://github.com/Skyspiriit/Amin-Live-TV/blob/main/VERIFICATION-0.3.0.md) for the channel-by-channel results.

Install it over 0.2.2 the same way as before. Settings and history are kept.
